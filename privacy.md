# Privacy Policy — alf_of

**Last updated:** September 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes what data the Discord application **alf_of** — short for **"Only Friends"** — (application ID 1476607300734291988, the "Service") collects, why, and how it is handled.

## 1. Data we collect

We only collect data that is necessary to provide the Service's functionality:

| Data | Source | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Discord user ID and username | Discord, when you use a command | Link your registered game accounts to you; send you direct-message notifications about them |
| Game account ID(s) and game session token(s) | Provided **voluntarily by you** via `/register` | Perform the daily sign-in claim and gift-code redemption you requested |
| Claim and redemption logs (result, timestamp) | Generated by the Service | Show your `/status` and `/history`; retry failed operations; troubleshoot |
| Account registration history | Generated by the Service | Enforce fair-use limits and prevent abuse |
| Server membership and roles (in memory) | Discord gateway (Guild Members intent) | Deliver DM notifications, apply role-based account limits, and deactivate registrations of users who left the server |
| Message content (in memory only) | Discord gateway (Message Content intent) | Detect and delete messages that accidentally expose a member's game UID in plain text (e.g. a mistyped `/register` command posted as a normal message) |
| Discord client language (locale, e.g. `es-419`) | Discord, when you use a slash command | Reply to you in your own language (for example, the notice explaining why a UID-exposing message was deleted) |

**Message content is never stored.** The UID-leak check inspects each message in memory only to decide whether it exposes a game UID, then discards it immediately. For deleted messages, only the author and message identifiers appear in short-lived operational logs — never the text. Content plays no other role anywhere in the Service. We do **not** collect presence data, voice data, or any data from users who never interact with the Service.

## 2. What we do NOT do

- We do **not** sell your data.
- We do **not** share your data with third parties for marketing or analytics.
- We do **not** use your data for advertising or profiling.

The only outbound use of your data is calling the game's official store API to perform the actions you explicitly requested (daily claims and gift-code redemptions for the accounts you registered).

## 3. Data retention

Retention is deliberately short and enforced by an automated weekly job:

- Detailed claim logs: deleted after **14 days**.
- Redemption records of expired/dead gift codes: deleted after **3 days**.
- Expired gift codes and their remaining redemption history: deleted after **60 days**.
- Dead registrations (accounts that never worked): deleted after **30 days**.
- Registered account data: kept until **you remove it** (`/remove`) or your registrations are deactivated (for example, after leaving the server).

## 4. Data storage and security

Data is stored in a private database on a server controlled by the Service administrator. Access is restricted to the administrator. Game session tokens are used only to call the game's official API on your behalf.

## 5. Your rights and choices

- **Access:** use `/status` and `/history` to see the data linked to you.
- **Deletion:** use `/remove` to delete a registered account, or contact the administrator (see below) to request full deletion of all data linked to your Discord ID.
- **Pause:** use `/pause` or `/stop` to suspend all automated actions at any time.

Deletion requests are honored within 30 days.

## 6. Children

The Service is not intended for users under 13 years of age (or the higher minimum age required by Discord in your country).

## 7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the latest revision. Material changes will be announced in the community server.

## 8. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the Service administrator through the community Discord server where the Service operates (a direct message to the server administrator, or any moderated support channel).
